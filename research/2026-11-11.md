### 1. SEC charges two entities in a $130M "risk-free" crypto-signals fraud
- **Why it matters to a beginner:** this is a textbook case of the "guaranteed returns + trading signals + fake registration" scam. If an offer says *risk-free*, that's the red flag.
- **Key facts (from source):** raised more than $130 million from tens of thousands of retail investors; promised "extraordinary, risk-free returns" from so-called SI-generated "signals" for crypto trading; falsely claimed SEC registration; active from at least Oct 2025 through June 2026.
- **Source:** https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-110-two-entities-charged-130-million-fraud-scheme-used-false-sec-filings-boost-investor-confidence · `research/raw/sec-130m-fraud.md`
- **Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Pillar:** 1 (Survival first)
- **Angle:** "Three words that mean you're about to get robbed." Lesson: check registration yourself on Investor.gov (the SEC names it in the release).

### 2. SEC fiscal 2026 enforcement results: ~$731M returned to 250,000+ harmed investors
