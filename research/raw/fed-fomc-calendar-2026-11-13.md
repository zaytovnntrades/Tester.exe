### Meeting calendars, statements, and minutes (2021-2027)

##### FOMC Search

* Search all FOMC materials
* [FOMC Document Filter](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/materials/)
* [Advanced Search](//www.fedsearch.org/fomc-docs/?advanced_search=true)

The FOMC holds eight regularly scheduled meetings during the year and other meetings as needed. Links to policy statements and minutes are in the calendars below. The minutes of regularly scheduled meetings are released three weeks after the date of the policy decision. Committee membership changes at the first regularly scheduled meeting of the year.

**FOIA**  
The FOMC makes an [annual report](/foia/fomc/annualreports.htm) pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act. The FOMC FOIA [Service Center](/foia/fomc/servicecenter.htm) provides information about the status of FOIA requests and the FOIA process.
