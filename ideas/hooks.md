Written by hand on 2026-11-12 (hook-generator isn't installed, and `breakdowns/` and `insights.md` are empty, so nothing here is tested yet). ★ = the one used in the script. Film two hooks per video and let the stats pick.

## 001 · 3 red flags from a $130M fraud case
1. ★ (number) Two words helped take over a hundred and thirty million dollars from everyday investors.
2. (question) Would you spot a $130 million scam if it landed in your DMs?
3. (mistake) The first red flag is the one beginners think is a green flag.
4. (bold claim) If a trading offer uses this one word, it's over. Leave.
5. (story) They said they were SEC registered. The SEC says that was false.
6. (contrarian) The riskiest thing in trading isn't the market.

## 002 · Why Fed day is for trading smaller
1. ★ (contrarian) On October twenty-eighth the Fed did nothing. That's exactly why beginners got hurt.
2. (number) Ten to two. That vote tells you more than the rate decision did.
3. (question) Why would the market move when the Fed changes nothing?
4. (mistake) Trading your normal size on Fed day is the mistake.
5. (bold claim) The best Fed-day trade for a beginner might be no trade.
6. (story) Two Fed officials wanted a hike. They were outvoted. Here's why that matters to you.

## 003 · Hike, hold, hold
1. ★ (number) Three central banks just made their call. Only one of them moved.
2. (question) Why did the pound and the euro get different news in the same week?
3. (contrarian) Forex isn't about charts first. It's about three buildings.
4. (bold claim) You can't understand a currency pair until you know who sets its interest rate.
5. (mistake) Beginners watch one currency. A pair is always two.
6. (story) October 28th: hold. October 29th: hold. November 5th: hike.
