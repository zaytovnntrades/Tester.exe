## 005 · How to read a candle

| Area | Score | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Hook | 7 | A question the viewer can answer in their head. It runs 5 seconds, and in the silent draft the ringed candle is too small to carry the first 2 seconds (fix G). |
| Retention | 7 | Each beat adds a new label to the same candle, which gives people a reason to stay. |
| Clarity | 7 | Beat 2 carries three things in 8 seconds: the body, open and close, and colour. It's the densest moment in the series. |
| Payoff | 8 | "Four prices. One shape." is a clean summary people can repeat. |
| CTA | 8 | Callback to the hook, plus fix E. |

**Fixes**
- **F. The caption says "in 36 seconds".** The final length will change once the voice is recorded. Change to "How to read a candlestick: open, high, low, close."
- **G. The ringed candle in the opening is too small.** In the draft it's a thin candle in a row of eleven. Make the ringed one a clear, tall candle so the hook reads on a phone at a glance.
- **Watch, don't fix yet: beat 2's pace.** If it feels rushed once the voice is on, move "Its colour tells you up or down" into its own short beat.

**Accuracy check:** body = open and close, wicks = high and low, one candle = one time period. All standard. The on-screen note says colours vary by platform, which is true.

## 007 · What leverage is

| Area | Score | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Hook | 6 | The turn ("That's the danger") doesn't land until about second 4.5. That's late for a hook. See fix H. |
| Retention | 8 | The magnifier, then margin, then the two gauges: each scene is a new picture. |
| Clarity | 8 | "Margin" is introduced in plain words before it's used. |
| Payoff | 8 | The loss bar growing past the deposit bar is the strongest image in the series. |
| CTA | 7 | "I'm on sim" is honest and on-brand. Plus fix E. |

**Fix**
- **H. Shorten the hook.** From "Leverage lets you control a big trade with a small account. That's the danger." (14 words) to "A small account can control a big trade. That's the danger." (11 words). The turn lands about a second earlier.

**Accuracy check:** "Losses can be bigger than your deposit" is true for futures. For forex it depends on the country and broker, which is why the line says "can be". Keep that wording.
