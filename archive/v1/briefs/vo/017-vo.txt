Two questions before every trade.

What can I lose? What can I make? Skip them, and you'll risk a lot to make a little.

Risk is the distance from your entry to your stop loss.

Reward is the distance from your entry to your target.

Compare them before you click. A small reward for a big risk is a trade to skip. Decide this on every trade, not just some.

Know your risk. Know your reward. Then decide. Survive first. Follow.
