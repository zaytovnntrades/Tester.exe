You clicked one price. Got another.

That gap has a name. And it costs you.

Slippage is the difference between the price you expected and the price you got.

It shows up when price moves fast. Like around big news, or when few traders are active.

It's part of trading. Calmer markets and limit orders can reduce it. Nothing removes it. So count it as a cost, every time.

Expect the gap. Plan for it. Survive first. Follow.
