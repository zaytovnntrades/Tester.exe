Price doesn't glide. It steps.

It moves in tiny steps. Each step is worth money. Not knowing how much is trading blind.

In futures, the smallest step is called a tick.

In forex, the common unit is called a pip.

Every market sets its own value per step. Look it up on the exchange or your platform before you trade it. With leverage, steps add up fast.

Know what one step costs you. Survive first. Follow.
