Thirty days. Five rules that matter.

Thirty days of basics, down to five rules. Forget the rest if you must. Don't forget these.

One. Exit before entry. Two. Smallest size.

Three. A max daily loss. Four. No plan, no trade.

Five. Learn it on sim before you risk a dollar. Every rule, practiced until it's boring.

None of them is about making money. All of them are about staying in the game long enough to learn. Survive first. Follow.
