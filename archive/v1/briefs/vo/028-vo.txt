Pilots use checklists. Traders need one.

Before every flight, every time. Without one, you decide everything in the heat of the moment.

One. Why am I entering? If you can't answer in a sentence, stop.

Two. Where is my exit if I'm wrong? Set it before you enter.

Three. How much am I risking? Decide the size last, not first. It has to fit your exit.

Three questions. Every trade. No answers, no trade. Survive first. Follow.
