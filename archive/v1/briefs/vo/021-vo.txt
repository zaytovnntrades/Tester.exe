Futures come in sizes. Know the smallest.

Start too big, and one ordinary move hurts.

A micro contract tracks the same market as the bigger one. It's just a fraction of the size.

Smaller size means each move in price is worth less. Wins and losses both shrink. That makes it a gentler place to learn.

It's still leveraged. Leverage still magnifies losses. Smaller doesn't mean safe.

Smallest size. On sim first. That's the path. Survive first. Follow.
