Those bars under your chart? That's volume.

They aren't decoration. Ignore them, and you can't tell a real move from a weak one.

Volume counts how much was traded in each candle. Tall bar, lots of trading.

A big move on high volume means many traders took part.

The same move on low volume had fewer traders behind it. Treat it with more doubt.

Price shows what happened. Volume shows how many showed up. Survive first. Follow.
