Trade without a plan, and you're guessing.

Guessing feels fine on good days. On bad days, it costs you money.

A trading plan is a written page. What you trade. When you trade. How much you risk.

It also says how you exit. Your stop loss. Your target. Your max daily loss.

Write it when you're calm. Follow it when you're not. Change it only after the market closes.

One page. Written down. Test it on sim. Survive first. Follow.
