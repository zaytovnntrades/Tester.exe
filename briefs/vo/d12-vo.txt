Plan your exit before your entry.

No exit plan? Then a small loss can grow into a big one. Fast.

A stop loss is an order you set before you enter. It closes your trade at a price you pick.

Price hits it? You're out. Small loss. You trade again tomorrow.

It's not a promise. In a fast market, price can jump past it. That's slippage. But it beats hoping.

Exit first. Entry second. Practice it on sim. Survive first. Follow.
