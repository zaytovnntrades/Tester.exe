Markets have rush hours.

And dead hours. Trade the dead ones, and you sit in slow, choppy price.

Many markets trade across time zones. Asia. Then Europe. Then America. Each region is a session.

When a big region opens, more traders show up. Moves tend to get bigger, and spreads often get smaller.

When two regions are open together, it's often the busiest time of the day. Busier can mean faster. Not safer.

Learn when your market is awake. Check its hours before you trade. Survive first. Follow.
