The most dangerous trade is the one right after a loss.

It's called revenge trading. You're not trading the market. You're trading your anger.

It starts the same way every time. A loss stings. You want it back. Now.

So you rush in. Bigger size. No plan. That's how one loss becomes three.

The fix is a rule made in advance. After a loss, you stop. Walk away. Come back later.

The market will be there tomorrow. Make sure your account is too. Survive first. Follow.
