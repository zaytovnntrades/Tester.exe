Click buy the wrong way, and you pay a price you never agreed to.

There are two basic order types. They do opposite jobs.

A market order says: fill me now. You get speed. You don't control the price.

A limit order says: fill me at this price or better. You control the price. It might never fill.

Speed or price. You pick one. Know which you picked before you click.

Market for speed. Limit for price. Practice both on sim. Survive first. Follow.
