One of the most useful trading tools is free. A notebook.

Without it, you repeat the same mistake and call it bad luck.

A trading journal is a record of every trade. Why you took it. What happened.

Add one line on how you felt. Calm. Rushed. Angry. That's where patterns hide.

Read it back every week. Your mistakes will repeat. Now you can see them.

Write it down. Sim trades count too. Survive first. Follow.
