Futures sound scary. They're agreements.

But they're leveraged ones. And leverage magnifies losses, so understand them first.

A futures contract is an agreement to buy or sell something at a set price on a set date. Traders usually close early and take the difference.

Each contract tracks a market. A stock index. Oil. Gold.

Many come in smaller micro sizes. Same market, a fraction of the size. Smaller, but still leveraged.

Smallest size. On sim first. Survive first. Follow.
