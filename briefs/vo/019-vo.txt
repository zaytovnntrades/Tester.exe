Every trader meets this word. Drawdown.

Ignore it, and a bad stretch quietly becomes a blown account.

Drawdown is how far your account has fallen from its highest point.

The deeper it gets, the harder the climb back. A big hole needs an even bigger gain to fill.

So traders set a limit. Reach it, and they stop and review. Before the hole gets deep.

Small drawdowns are normal. Deep ones end accounts. Survive first. Follow.
