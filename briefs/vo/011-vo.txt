There are two prices for everything you trade. Not one.

The gap between them is a cost. You pay it on every trade.

The bid is the highest price a buyer is offering right now.

The ask is the lowest price a seller will accept right now.

The space between them is the spread. Buy, then sell straight away, and you lose that gap.

Bid. Ask. Spread. Know your cost before you click. Survive first. Follow.
