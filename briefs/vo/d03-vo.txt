Every trade has two prices. Not one.

Miss the gap between them, and you pay a cost on every trade without noticing.

The bid is the highest price a buyer is offering right now.

The ask is the lowest price a seller will accept right now.

The space between them is the spread. Buy, then sell straight away, and you lose that gap. Busy markets usually have smaller spreads.

Bid. Ask. Spread. Know your cost before you click. Survive first. Follow.
