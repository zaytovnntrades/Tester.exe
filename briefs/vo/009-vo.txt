Price has floors and ceilings. Nobody draws them for you.

Miss them, and you enter right where price tends to turn.

Support is a floor. An area where price fell, stopped, and bounced before.

Resistance is a ceiling. An area where price rose, stalled, and dropped before.

They're zones, not exact lines. And they break. Nothing holds forever.

Find the floor. Find the ceiling. Then decide. Survive first. Follow.
