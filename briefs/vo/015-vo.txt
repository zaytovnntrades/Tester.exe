Don't learn to trade with real money.

You wouldn't learn to drive on a motorway. Every beginner mistake costs money. Unless you make it on sim.

Sim is a practice account. The same charts. Pretend money.

Use it to practice one thing at a time. Entries. Stops. Size.

Sim won't feel like real money. Your emotions stay quieter. But it shows whether you can follow your own rules.

I'm on sim right now. Start there. Survive first. Follow.
