Someone is always on the other side.

When you buy, someone sells to you. Forget that, and you'll think price moves by magic.

A market is just a place where buyers and sellers meet. Today that place is mostly a screen.

Price moves when one side is more eager. Eager buyers push price up. Eager sellers push it down.

You're not trading against a chart. You're trading against other people. Many of them are professionals.

Respect the other side. Learn before you risk money. Survive first. Follow.
