The market has rush hours. And dead hours.

Trade the dead ones, and you sit in slow, choppy price.

Currencies trade around the clock on weekdays, passing between regions. Asia. Then Europe. Then America.

When a big region opens, more traders show up. Moves tend to get bigger.

When two regions are open together, it's often the busiest time of the day.

Learn when your market is awake. Survive first. Follow.
