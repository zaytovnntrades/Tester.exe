Not where to enter. How much.

That's the biggest decision in a trade. Too big, and one normal loss does real damage to your account.

Position size is how much you trade. How many shares or contracts.

Bigger size means every move in price counts for more. Up and down.

So decide the loss you can accept first. Set your stop loss. Then size the trade to fit it. Not the other way round.

Start with the smallest size there is. On sim. Survive first. Follow.
