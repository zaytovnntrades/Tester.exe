Price has floors and ceilings.

Nobody draws them for you. Miss them, and you enter right where price tends to turn.

Support is a floor. An area where price fell, stopped, and bounced before.

Resistance is a ceiling. An area where price rose, stalled, and dropped before.

They're zones, not exact lines. Price can poke through and come back. And they do break. Nothing holds forever.

Find the floor. Find the ceiling. Then decide. Survive first. Follow.
