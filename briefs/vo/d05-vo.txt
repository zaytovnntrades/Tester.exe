Same market. Different stories.

Pick the wrong timeframe, and a small dip can look like a crash.

A timeframe is how much time each candle covers. One minute. One hour. One day. You choose it.

Short timeframes show every wiggle. Lots of noise. Lots of false alarms.

Long timeframes show the bigger direction. Many traders check the bigger one first. Then they zoom in.

Pick your timeframe on purpose. Practice it on sim. Survive first. Follow.
