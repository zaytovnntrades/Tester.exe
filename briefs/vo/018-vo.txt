Win most trades. Still lose money.

Because win rate is only half the picture.

Win rate is how often you win. Nothing more. It says nothing about how much.

The other half is size. How big are the wins? How big are the losses? You need both answers.

Small wins and one big loss can wipe out a whole week. That's why the stop loss matters.

Stop asking how often. Start asking how much. Survive first. Follow.
