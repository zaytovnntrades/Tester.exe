Know where you'll take profit.

Without a plan, traders hold winners too long and watch them turn into losers.

A target is the price where you plan to close a winning trade. Many traders set it with a take-profit order.

Set it before you enter, at the same time as your stop loss. Exit for a loss. Exit for a win. Both planned.

Price may never reach it. That's fine. A target is a plan, not a promise.

Two exits before every entry. Practice it on sim. Survive first. Follow.
