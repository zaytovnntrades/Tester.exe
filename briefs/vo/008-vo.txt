Before anything else, answer one question. Which way is the market going?

Trade against it, and you're swimming upstream.

An uptrend makes higher highs and higher lows. Each push goes further than the last.

A downtrend is the mirror. Lower highs. Lower lows.

No clear steps either way? That's sideways. It's where forced trades go to die.

Up. Down. Sideways. Name it before you trade it. Survive first. Follow.
