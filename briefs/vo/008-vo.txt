Which way is the market going?

Answer that before anything else. Trade against it, and you're swimming upstream.

An uptrend makes higher highs and higher lows. Each push goes further than the last. Each dip stops higher.

A downtrend is the mirror. Lower highs. Lower lows.

No clear steps either way? That's sideways. Price just chops back and forth. It's where forced trades go to die.

Up. Down. Sideways. Name it before you trade it. Survive first. Follow.
