The biggest decision in a trade isn't where to enter. It's how much.

Too big, and one normal loss does real damage to your account.

Position size is how much you trade. One contract. Two. Ten.

Bigger size means every move in price counts for more. Up and down.

So decide the loss you can accept first. Then size the trade to fit it. Not the other way round.

Start with the smallest size there is. On sim. Survive first. Follow.
