Futures come in sizes. Start by knowing the smallest one.

Start too big, and one ordinary move hurts.

A micro contract tracks the same market as the bigger one. It's just a fraction of the size.

Smaller size means each move in price is worth less. Wins and losses both shrink.

It's still leveraged. Leverage still magnifies losses. Smaller doesn't mean safe.
