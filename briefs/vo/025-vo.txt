Price is flying. Everyone's talking about it. Your finger is on the button.

That feeling is FOMO. Fear of missing out. It's a terrible entry signal.

By the time a move feels obvious, a lot of it has already happened.

Chasing means entering far from any sensible exit. Your risk gets bigger.

So here's the rule. No plan before the move? No trade during it.

There's always another trade. There isn't always another account. Survive first. Follow.
