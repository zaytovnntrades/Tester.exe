Can you read this shape?

It's called a candle. It's on almost every chart. If you can't read it, you're guessing.

One candle covers one slice of time. Inside it are four prices.

The thick part is the body. It shows where price opened and where it closed. Green means it closed higher. Red means it closed lower.

The thin lines are wicks. They show the highest and lowest price in that time. Colours vary by platform, so check yours.

Open. High. Low. Close. Four prices, one shape. Survive first. Follow.
