More trades doesn't mean more money. Often it means more mistakes and more costs.

Every trade has a cost. And every trade is a chance to break your rules.

Overtrading is trading because you're bored. Or behind. Or itching to click.

The warning sign is simple. You can't say why you took the trade.

The fix is a limit. A set number of trades per day. Decided before the open.

Fewer trades. Better trades. Survive first. Follow.
