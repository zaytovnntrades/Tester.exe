You can trade a falling market.

It's called going short. Mix up the two directions, and you're working against yourself.

Going long means you buy first. You gain if price rises. You lose if it falls.

Going short means you sell first. You gain if price falls. You lose if it rises.

Either way, both directions can lose. Know which one you're in before you click.

Long is up. Short is down. Practice both on sim. Survive first. Follow.
