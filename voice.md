# Voice: the blunt coach who's still in the gym

Built by hand on 2026-11-12 from your answers: **blunt coach** tone, **learning on sim**, **faceless with AI voice**. No writing samples yet, so treat this as version 1.

## The one-line voice
A tough coach who is honest that they're still training. Blunt about the rules, never about the viewer, and never pretending to be a pro.

## Why the tone needs one adjustment
"Blunt coach" usually leans on authority: "I've done this for years, listen to me." You're on sim, so that authority has to come from somewhere else:
1. **The rules themselves** ("Every real trade has risk. Full stop.")
2. **Official sources** ("The SEC says…", "The Fed's own statement…")
3. **Going first** ("I'm on sim. You should be too.")

## Rules
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Short sentences. 5 to 10 words is the target. | Long wind-ups. No "so today I want to talk about". |
| Commands for habits: "Cut your size." "Stay on sim." | Commands for trades: never "buy", "sell", "get in" (§6.1). |
| Call out the mistake, not the person: "That's the mistake." | Insults: no "idiot", "dumb money", "you're gambling". |
| Say "you" and "your account". | "Guys", "fam", "traders!" openers. |
| Plain words: "lose", "blow up", "smaller". | Jargon without a 3-word explanation. |
| One hard truth per video, said once, clearly. | Hype words. The §6.2 list is banned: guaranteed, risk-free, easy money, get rich, can't lose, quit your job. |
| Admit the level: "I'm on sim." | Implied results or live experience. |

## Rhythm (matters more with an AI voice)
- Write for the ear. One idea per sentence.
- Use full stops for pauses. AI voices rush through commas.
- Spell out numbers the way they should be spoken: "three and three-quarters percent", "ten to two".
- Avoid symbols and tickers the voice might misread. Write "M-E-S" guidance in the brief if needed.
- End each beat on the strong word: "…selling, not trading."

## Signature moves
- **The flat verdict:** a two- or three-word sentence after a fact. "That's the trap." "Size kills."
- **Survive first:** the phrase closes most videos.
- **Sim honesty:** at least every few videos, one line that says you're on sim.

## Before and after
- Before: "A split vote means the next decision is less certain, and uncertainty can move price fast in both directions."
- After: "The vote split. Ten to two. Split votes mean nobody's sure. Unsure markets swing hard. Both ways."

- Before: "So on scheduled event days, the survival move isn't guessing direction. It's trading smaller, or staying on sim."
- After: "Fed day isn't for guessing. Cut your size. Or stay on sim. I'm on sim."
