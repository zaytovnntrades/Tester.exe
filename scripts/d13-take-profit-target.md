LESSON (8–32s): 3 beats, one idea each
  Beat 1:
  VO: A target is the price where you plan to close a winning trade. Many traders set it with a take-profit order.
  ON-SCREEN: Target · where you plan to take profit · TARGET · ENTRY · STOP LOSS · TO TARGET
  VISUAL: Target (green), entry and stop loss (red), with a bracket marking the distance (illustration).

  Beat 2:
  VO: Set it before you enter, at the same time as your stop loss. Exit for a loss. Exit for a win. Both planned.

  VISUAL: Target (green), entry and stop loss (red), with a bracket marking the distance (illustration).
